This document explains what information of yours may be collected by **brightseed.** when you access the website www.brightseed.io (the “website”). It also describes how information will be used and how you can get in touch with us if you need help on how to control your data. This Privacy Policy is incorporated into, and is subject to, the [Terms of Use](/terms) document available on this website. Please read this document carefully to understand how we process your data before browsing this website.

  

## Information That We Obtain About You

  

You can contact **brightseed.** by email at [**contact@brightseed.io**](mailto:contact@brightseed.io). Email links on this website open your chosen email application. Clicking a link does not send a message; you choose whether to send it using your email service.

  

When you send us an email, we receive the information you include in your correspondence, which may include:

  

-   Your name, if provided
-   Your email address
-   Your message subject and content
-   Any attachments or other information you choose to share

  

We use this correspondence to respond to your enquiry, discuss your project, and provide requested support. You choose what information to include in your email. Your email provider handles sending your message under its own privacy policy.

  

## Contact Forms

When the contact form is available, we use Web3Forms to receive enquiries. When you submit it, your name, email address and message are sent to Web3Forms so it can deliver the enquiry to our inbox. We use these details to respond to you and discuss your project. Contact enquiries do not subscribe you to marketing emails. You can also contact us directly by email.

## Newsletter Signups

When you subscribe through our newsletter form, your email address is sent to MailerLite to manage your subscription and send Brightseed updates about websites, web applications and AI. Follow the confirmation instructions shown by MailerLite to complete your signup. You can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter or contact us at [contact@brightseed.io](mailto:contact@brightseed.io).

## Cookies

  

Google Analytics is not currently enabled on this website. We plan to introduce analytics and will update this policy before it is enabled to explain what information is collected, how it is used and the choices available to visitors.

  

Cookies can be used to provide you with tailored information from a website. Your web browser may have settings which will notify you of cookie placement requests or decline cookies completely. You can delete the files that contain cookies; those files are stored as part of your internet browser.

  

## How Collected Data Is Used

  

**brightseed.** uses the collected data for various purposes:

  

-   To provide and maintain the service
-   To notify you about changes to our service
-   To provide customer care and support
-   To detect, prevent and address technical issues

  

## Your Rights

  

If you wish to access or exclude any personal data that we hold about you or to request that we delete any information about you, you may contact us as set forth in the Contacting Us section. At any time, you may also object to the processing of your personal data or ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data.  

  

## Children’s Rights

  

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16. If you are under 16 years of age, then please do not use or access the website at any time or in any manner.  

  

## Third-Party Services

  

We use [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/privacy) to process contact-form submissions and [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy) to manage newsletter signups and emails. These services receive the information submitted through their respective forms and handle it under their privacy policies. We do not load MailerLite's universal tracking script on this website.

The **brightseed** website links to external websites and social profiles. When you follow one of these links, the destination service handles your visit under its own privacy policy. We do not receive access to your social accounts through these links.

  

## Changes To Our Privacy Policy

  

We reserve the right to change this Privacy Policy at any time at our sole discretion. If we make changes to this Privacy Policy they will be posted on the website so that you are always aware of what information we gather, how we might use that information and in what circumstances we may disclose it.
